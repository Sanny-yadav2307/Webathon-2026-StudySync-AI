# 🧠 StudySync AI (Webathon 2026)

Transform your study materials into smart summaries, interactive quizzes, instant chat, and study notes powered by Google Gemini AI. Built for WebNova / Webathon 2026.

## 🚀 Live Links
* **Frontend (Vercel):** [https://webathon-2026-study-sync-ai.vercel.app](https://webathon-2026-study-sync-ai.vercel.app)
* **Backend API (Render):** [https://studysync-backend-a3zj.onrender.com](https://studysync-backend-a3zj.onrender.com)
* **API Documentation:** [https://studysync-backend-a3zj.onrender.com/docs](https://studysync-backend-a3zj.onrender.com/docs)

---

## ✨ Key Features
* **📄 Smart Executive Summary:** Upload any study material or PDF, and get a concise, structured executive summary instantly.
* **📝 Interactive Mock Test Quiz (MCQs):** Automatically generates multiple-choice questions based on your document with instant evaluation and explanations.
* **💬 AI Document Chat Assistant:** Have an interactive conversation with your study document. Ask any question and get precise answers derived directly from your notes.
* **📥 Study Notes Download:** Easily download your generated summaries and notes for offline learning.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS (Hosted on Vercel)
* **Backend:** FastAPI, Python, Uvicorn, PyPDF, python-multipart (Hosted on Render)
* **AI Engine:** Google Gemini AI Model via `google-genai` SDK
